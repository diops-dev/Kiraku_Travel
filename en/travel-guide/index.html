PRACTICAL GUIDE

The travel toolkit.

Transport, the Suica card, table manners, and enough to decipher a shop sign. What you need to know before you leave, and what we prepare for you on arrival.

駅

The metro and the Shinkansen

The network is methodical once you have the key to it: colour-coded lines, arrowed interchanges, numbered platforms. The Shinkansen links the major cities at more than 300 km/h, with a punctuality that has become legendary. We explain how to book, where to stand on the platform, and how to string connections together without stress.

円

The Suica card

One card, and Japanese daily life opens up: metro, buses, trains, vending machines, konbini, luggage lockers. You top it up at a machine in seconds and off you go. We have it ready for you the moment you arrive.

食

Chopsticks

Holding them is not so difficult; the right reflexes are a little harder. Never stand them upright in the rice, never pass food from hand to hand with them, never point with them. A few simple rules, and a meal that passes without a hitch.
