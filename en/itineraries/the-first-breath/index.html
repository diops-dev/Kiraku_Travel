The First Breath

Tokyo then Kyoto, in one week. The discovery format for a first contact with Japan, focused on two cities so nothing is rushed.

01 / 03 · Tokyo de nuit

A flight arrangement designed to save half a day over one week

02

THE DETAIL OF THE STAY

Seven days, six nights.

· two cities, never in a hurry
01DAY

Arrival in Tokyo

Transfer and check-in. The afternoon unfolds gently in the hotel district.

Arrival
2-3DAY

Tokyo

Two days in the capital: contemporary Tokyo and the Tokyo of Edo, Asakusa and Akihabara.

TokyoAsakusa
04DAY

To Kyoto

Shinkansen to Kyoto in the morning. A first approach to the city in the afternoon, around the station and the hotel.

Shinkansen
05DAY

Kyoto

A full day, Fushimi Inari and southern Higashiyama, or a more active route depending on the mood of the moment.

Fushimi Inari
06DAY

Kyoto, Nara or Osaka

The last day is decided on the spot: a third day in Kyoto, Arashiyama and Kinkakuji, Osaka thirty minutes away by train, or Nara and its park of free-roaming deer.

Choice of your ownNara
07DAY

Departure via Kansai

From Kyoto, on to Kansai airport, about an hour and twenty minutes. End of the circuit.
