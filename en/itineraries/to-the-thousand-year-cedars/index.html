LONG ITINERARY · CL-08 · MIYAZAKI

To the Thousand Year Cedars

The most adventurous circuit in the catalogue: Kyoto, Hiroshima, the onsen towns of Kyushu, the sand baths of Ibusuki, then three nights hiking in the primeval forest of Yakushima.

01 / 04 · Une ruelle de Kyoto
YOUR STAY IN DETAIL

The points that matter.

· To the Thousand Year Cedars · twenty two days
01

Three nights hiking on Yakushima, a primeval forest and World Heritage site

02

The Jomon Sugi, a thousand-year cedar estimated to be between two and seven thousand years old

03

The hot sand baths of Ibusuki, buried to the neck in volcanic sand

04

The onsen of Beppu and the hot spring town of Yufuin, at the foot of Mount Yufu

05

A free day in Kumamoto to catch your breath before Ibusuki and Yakushima

06

A circuit best suited to an active, nature-loving traveller

THE DETAIL OF THE STAY

Twenty-two days, twenty-one nights.

· all the way to the forest that inspired Princess Mononoke
01DAY

Arrival in Kyoto

Connection from Kansai airport by the Haruka express. Check-in, an afternoon in the hotel district.

Arrival
2-6DAY

Kyoto and its surroundings

Five days in Kyoto, with the option of also visiting Osaka, Nara and Kobe, all under an hour away, without changing hotel.

Kyoto
07DAY

To Kurashiki

Shinkansen then local train to Kurashiki: the Bikan district, its canals, its white warehouses, the Ohara Museum of Art.

Kurashiki
08DAY

To Hiroshima

More of Kurashiki, then departure for Hiroshima in the late afternoon. First okonomiyaki dinner.

Hiroshima
09DAY

Hiroshima

The city centre, the castle, the Peace Memorial Park.

Peace Memorial
10DAY

Miyajima

Itsukushima Shrine and its torii standing in the water at high tide.

Miyajima
11DAY

To Beppu

Train to Beppu, Japan's onsen capital. Discovering the eight hells, bubbling springs in surprising colours.

BeppuOnsen
12DAY

To Yufuin

Bus or sightseeing train to Yufuin, a hot spring town at the foot of Mount Yufu, craft lanes and Lake Kinrin.

Yufuin
13DAY

To Kumamoto

Train or bus to Kumamoto. In the afternoon, Kumamoto Castle, one of Japan's three great castles.

Kumamoto Castle
14DAY

Kumamoto, free day

A day deliberately left free before heading south: Suizenji Garden, the Kamitori and Shimotori districts, local food.

Free day
15DAY

Ibusuki

The hot sand baths of the Saraku onsen, buried to the neck in volcanic sand. At low tide, Chiringashima island, linked to the mainland by a fleeting sandbar.

Sand baths
16-19DAY

Yakushima

Boat from Ibusuki to the island, a primeval forest and World Heritage site. Hikes to Shiratani Unsuikyo, the mossy forest that inspired Princess Mononoke, and to the Jomon Sugi, a thousand-year cedar.

YakushimaHikingPrimeval forest
20-21DAY

Osaka

Return to Osaka by train or plane. Two days in the city: the castle, Shinsekai, Dotonbori.

Osaka
22DAY

Departure via Kansai

On to Kansai airport. End of the circuit.

Departure
