LONG ITINERARY · SPIRITUAL

A Thousand Steps North

Tokyo, then the archipelago's climb north through Tohoku and Hokkaido: Yamadera and its thousand steps, Hirosaki, Hakodate and its bay, Sapporo. Japan away from the classic triangle.

01 / 04 · Tokyo de nuit
YOUR STAY IN DETAIL

The points that matter.

· A Thousand Steps North · fourteen days
01

The mountain temple of Yamadera and its over one thousand steps, founded in 860

02

Hirosaki and its castle, one of the three finest cherry blossom sites in Japan

03

The night view from Mount Hakodate, considered one of the three finest in Japan

04

Goryokaku Park, Japan's only star-shaped fortress

05

Sapporo, its fish market and Moerenuma Park, a work by sculptor Isamu Noguchi

06

A Japan different from the Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka triangle, relevant all year round

THE DETAIL OF THE STAY

Fourteen days, thirteen nights.

· six nights in Tokyo, then a steady climb north
01DAY

Arrival in Tokyo

Transfer from the airport and check-in. The afternoon unfolds gently in the hotel district.

Arrival
2-6DAY

Tokyo and its surroundings

Five days in Tokyo, shaped to your taste: contemporary Tokyo, the Tokyo of Edo, the bay, and an excursion of your choice on the outskirts, Kamakura, Takaosan or Nikko.

TokyoExcursion of choice
07DAY

To Sendai and Yamadera temple

Train to Sendai, then on to Yamadera temple, set on a mountainside, founded in 860. Over a thousand steps to reach it, one of the finest sacred landscapes in Tohoku.

YamaderaA thousand steps
08DAY

To Aomori

Train to Aomori, at the northern tip of Honshu. The rest of the day is devoted to exploring the city.

Aomori
09DAY

Hirosaki

A short half-hour train ride to Hirosaki: its castle and park, one of the three finest cherry blossom sites in Japan.

Hirosaki Castle
10DAY

To Hakodate

Train through the Seikan Tunnel, under the Tsugaru Strait. The bay and its red-brick warehouses, Goryokaku Park, Japan's only star-shaped fortress, then the climb up Mount Hakodate for one of the finest night views in the country.

GoryokakuNight view
11DAY

To Sapporo

Train to Hokkaido's capital. Check-in and a first look at the city centre.

Sapporo
12DAY

Sapporo

Moerenuma Park, a total work by sculptor Isamu Noguchi, the Jogai Ichiba fish market, then the climb up Mount Moiwa for a panorama over the city.

Moerenuma ParkFish market
13DAY

Back to Tokyo

Return to Tokyo, by train or by air depending on how the trip is arranged.

Return
14DAY

Departure

Transfer to the airport. End of the circuit.

Departure
