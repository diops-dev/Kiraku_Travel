LONG ITINERARY · CULTURE

From Neon to Silence

Tokyo, Mount Fuji, five nights in Kyoto that are never the same twice, then Osaka and Mount Shigi. The entry-point circuit into the archipelago, in its most complete version.

01 / 04 · Tokyo de nuit
YOUR STAY IN DETAIL

The points that matter.

· From Neon to Silence · fourteen days
01

Five nights in Kyoto, never the same Kyoto twice: south of the city, Higashiyama, Ryoanji and Arashiyama, the Imperial Palace, then Kurama and Kibune

02

One night in a ryokan with an onsen at the foot of Mount Fuji, in Kawaguchiko

03

A spiritual excursion to Mount Shigi from Osaka, without changing hotel

04

A Suica card handed over on arrival, and luggage forwarded between cities by takkyubin

05

A calligraphy workshop and a tea ceremony in Kyoto

06

A fully free day in Tokyo, before the flight home

THE DETAIL OF THE STAY

Fourteen days, thirteen nights.

· three cities, a ryokan at the foot of Fuji, and five faces of Kyoto
01DAY

Arrival in Tokyo

Transfer to the city centre, check-in and the Suica card handed over. First dinner in Shinjuku, then an hour of night-time discovery: Kabukicho, Golden Gai, Omoide Yokocho.

ArrivalShinjuku by night
02DAY

Tokyo, the face of Edo

Asakusa and the Sensoji temple, Ameyoko, Ueno Park and its national museum or the Yanesen district, then Akihabara and its popular culture.

AsakusaUenoAkihabara
03DAY

Tokyo, the contemporary face

Meiji Shrine, Takeshita dori and Harajuku, Omotesando, then Shibuya: Miyashita Park, Hachiko and the famous crossing.

HarajukuShibuya
04DAY

To Mount Fuji and Kawaguchiko

Direct train to Kawaguchiko, at the foot of Fuji. A walk around the lake, the ride up Mount Tenjo by ropeway, then an onsen and a Japanese dinner at the ryokan.

Mount FujiRyokanOnsen
05DAY

From Kawaguchiko to Kyoto

Bus then shinkansen to Kyoto, a bento lunch on board. The afternoon starts strong: Tofukuji, then Fushimi Inari Taisha and its tunnel of torii.

ShinkansenFushimi Inari
06DAY

Kyoto, Higashiyama and Gion

Ginkakuji and the Philosopher's Path, then Higashiyama: Entokuin, the Ryozen Kannon statue, the Kawai Kanjiro Memorial Museum and Kenninji temple, walking between each site through the lanes of Gion.

HigashiyamaGion
07DAY

Kyoto, Ryoanji and Arashiyama

The zen garden of Ryoanji, the imperial temple of Ninnaji, then the afternoon in Arashiyama: the bamboo grove, Okochi Sanso, Tenryuji and the Togetsukyo bridge.

RyoanjiArashiyama
08DAY

Kyoto, the Imperial Palace

A slower day: the Imperial Palace gardens, a tea ceremony and an introduction to calligraphy, then a guided visit of the Sento Imperial Palace in the afternoon.

Imperial PalaceTea ceremony
09DAY

Kyoto, excursion to Kurama and Kibune

A day off the classic routes in the mountains north of Kyoto: Kurama dera temple, the forest, then the walk to Kibune and its riverside shrine.

HikeKurama
10DAY

To Osaka

Osaka Castle, Kuromon Market for lunch, then Shinsekai and its Tsutenkaku tower, and finally Dotonbori for dinner under the neon signs.

Osaka CastleDotonbori
11DAY

Excursion to Mount Shigi

A day at Chogosonshiji temple, known for its huge tiger and its tunnel of torii, then a walk on the paths of Mount Shigi, between forests and small shrines.

Mount ShigiGentle hike
12DAY

Back to Tokyo

Shinkansen back to Tokyo, check-in at the hotel then a free late afternoon in a district not yet explored, depending on the mood of the moment.

Shinkansen
13DAY

Tokyo, free day

A day deliberately left free after a dense circuit: museums, Yanaka, Daikanyama, shopping in Ginza or Shibuya. Farewell dinner in the evening.

Free dayFarewell dinner
14DAY

Departure

Transfer to the airport, Haneda or Narita depending on the flight. End of the circuit.
