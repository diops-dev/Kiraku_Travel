OUR APPROACH

What we do, what we do not do.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

We do not promise you a secret Japan. Japan does not need to be hidden to be lived differently.

We do not put you in a ryokan that has kept the name but serves its breakfast as a buffet.

We do not send you sightseeing in a group, at the hours when everyone else is there.

WHAT WE DO

We take you to places few travellers see.

We sit you down at the tables where the Japanese themselves eat.

We show you how the Japanese live from day to day.

We let you truly take on the culture: religion, philosophy, work, food, transport.

We travel by train, because that is how Japan was designed.

We visit at daybreak, before the groups.

“It is slow. It is expensive, for us. But it is the only way to be sure that Hirosan will still be there, and that she will serve breakfast at seven.”
楽
OUR RULES

Three rules we set ourselves.

01 ·

Eight people maximum, in private as in a group.

One to eight travellers in private, four to eight in a small group. Beyond that, it is no longer the same journey.

02 ·

The family ryokan, the minshuku: whenever it is possible.

It depends on the stops and the season. We do not promise for eight what we can only hold for two, and we tell you which is which before you leave.

03 ·

Every journey is rewoven around you.

Your pace, your tastes, your season. Nothing is standardised.

OUR WAYS OF TRAVELLING

Two ways to go.

The private journey.

No fixed dates. You leave when you like, for as long as you like. We draw the route around your wishes, your pace, your season.
