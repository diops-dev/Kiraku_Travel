Give your employees a trip they will still be talking about years from now.

THE AGENCY

A small licensed agency that scouts its own itineraries.

Kiraku Travel is an independent agency based in Paris, registered with Atout France under number IM075260052. We do not resell tours bought elsewhere: every itinerary is scouted on the ground, season by season, inn by inn.

Our three principles take very few words. Small groups, so the place stays alive. A slow pace, because a journey needs time to settle. Honest pricing, everything is written down before you sign.

WHAT WE BRING TO YOUR WORKS COUNCIL

The committee keeps control, we take on the logistics.

01
A negotiated offer, at no extra cost to the council

Group rates on our signature itineraries, with exactly the same service as our individual travellers receive. No booking fee charged to the committee.

02
One contact from the first exchange to the return

Someone who knows your file, reachable by telephone, who follows the sign ups, the deposits and the unexpected.

03
Materials ready to share

Posters, itinerary sheets, copy for your intranet and your newsletter. We prepare the internal communication with you.

04
Flexible ticketing

Staggered departures, payment in instalments, a council contribution that can be adjusted per employee or per household.

THE BENEFITS FOR YOUR EMPLOYEES

A trip that does not feel like a corporate trip.

01
Groups of four to eight people

Never a coach, never a flag. Trains, family run inns, tables where you sit down with local people.

02
An English speaking guide on the ground

A guide who lives in Japan or in Peru, available throughout the stay and reachable outside the activities.

03
Everything is included, even what goes unnoticed

Transfers, entrance fees, domestic transport, twenty four hour assistance and cancellation insurance.

04
A trip worth telling on the way home

Employees come home with a printed travel journal, their photographs and plenty to talk about for months.
